# Dual-use software disclosure

`@echovisionlab/youtube-audio` resolves and proxies authenticated access to
YouTube audio sources. This capability can be used for legitimate workflows,
including processing media owned by the user or covered by permission, license,
or another lawful basis. It can also be misused to copy protected media without
authorization.

Integrators must restrict access to authenticated users, obtain all necessary
copyright and rights clearances, and comply with YouTube's terms and applicable
law. Echo Vision Lab does not encourage infringement, piracy, or unauthorized
copying.
